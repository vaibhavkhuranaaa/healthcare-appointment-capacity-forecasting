# Scope

## In scope

- Public recorded GP appointments and separate telephone, online, workforce,
  population, experience, deprivation, and respiratory context.
- Daily 1–28 day sub-ICB forecast distributions and monthly practice drill-down.
- Filterable, bounded source rows at their lowest published grain.
- Deterministic, non-persistent hypothetical capacity schedules.

## Out of scope

- Actual capacity, utilisation, slots, rosters, cancellations, workload, or combined total demand.
- Patient identifiers, free text, clinical diagnosis, triage, treatment, or medical-device use.
- Secure four-week GPAD as a launch dependency or public asset.
- Commercial use, NHS branding, or implied NHS endorsement.

The current v0 remains active until the replacement passes all private and deployment gates.
