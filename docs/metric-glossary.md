# Metric glossary

## Recorded appointments

Publisher-counted GPAD appointments. Attended, DNA, and Unknown are preserved. This is
recorded activity, not capacity or workload.

## WAPE

Sum of absolute forecast errors divided by the sum of observed recorded appointments.

## MASE

Mean absolute forecast error divided by the in-sample naive-error scale. Values below
1 outperform that scale.

## Interval coverage

Share of observed values between p10 and p90. The nominal interval is 80%.

## Median gap

Forecast p50 minus visitor-entered hypothetical capacity.

## p90 risk gap

Forecast p90 minus visitor-entered hypothetical capacity. A positive value creates a
review flag, not a staffing recommendation.
